  Niveau Tech : 3 (Iron Age / Late Roman)
  Description : Felt-padded protective garment, sometimes covered with leather for waterproofing. Designed to cushion the body from the weight and friction of heavy metal armor. Mentioned in late Roman military treatises as essential under armor for both infantry and cavalry.

  Niveau Tech : 4 (High Middle Ages)
  Description : Classic padded armor made of layered linen or cotton (from Arabic al-qutn). Lighter and more fitted than later gambesons. Primarily worn under chainmail, but can also serve as light standalone protection for archers and light troops in the 12th-13th centuries.

Gambeson
  Prix : 42 sp
  Niveau Tech : 4 (Early to Late Middle Ages)
  Description : Simple thick padded tunic made of layered linen and wool. The most basic and affordable version of textile armor. Good entry-level protection that can be worn alone or under mail. The standard protective garment for common soldiers and men-at-arms across Europe from the 11th to 14th centuries.

Reinforced Gambeson
  Prix : 65 sp
  Niveau Tech : 5 (Late Middle Ages)
  Description : Classic thick and well-quilted gambeson made of linen and wool with dense stitching. The most common and versatile model used across Europe in the 14th century. Can be worn standalone by infantry or as excellent under-armor padding beneath plate harnesses.

Padded Jack
  Prix : 85 sp
  Niveau Tech : 5 (Late Middle Ages to Early Renaissance)
  Description : Heavier and more rigid padded armor with many layers (often 18-30+). Usually sleeved and sometimes reinforced. Often worn alone by infantry, archers, and billmen. Offers significantly better standalone protection than a standard gambeson against cuts and blunt trauma.

Jack of Plates
  Prix : 125 sp
  Niveau Tech : 7 (Renaissance)
  Description : Padded jack with small overlapping iron plates sewn inside the lining between layers of fabric. Excellent protection while remaining relatively flexible and comfortable. Brigandine
  Prix : 145 sp
  Niveau Tech : 5 (Late Middle Ages to Renaissance)
  Description : Fabric coat with small overlapping metal plates riveted to the inside of the garment. Much better protection against thrusts, arrows, and sword cuts than pure padded armor. Visually distinct from other textile armors due to the visible rivet pattern on the outside. Worn by men-at-arms and wealthy common soldiers from the 14th to 16th centuries.

Arming Doublet
  Prix : 95 sp
  Niveau Tech : 7 (Late Renaissance)
  Description : Finely tailored and structured garment designed specifically to be worn under a full plate harness. Features lacing points (points) to attach armor plates directly to the body. Less padded than earlier gambesons, more technical in construction. Essential for 16th century full plate wearers to distribute weight and prevent chafing.

Club, Great

Atlatl

  Niveau Tech : 0 (Stone Age)

Dagger, Bronze

Axe, Bronze
  Prix : 20 sp

Sword, Bronze
  Prix : 38 sp
  Niveau Tech : 2 (Bronze Age)
  Description : Bronze short sword or long dagger. Rare and precious due to the difficulty and cost of casting a functional sword-length blade. Reserved for wealthy warriors and leaders. A sign of high status as much as a practical weapon.

Pilum, Roman
  Prix : 35 sp

Verutum, Roman Light
  Prix : 11 sp

Javelin, Iron Leaf-Shaped
  Prix : 14 sp
  Niveau Tech : 3 (Iron Age)
  Description : Iron javelin with a leaf-shaped blade and central midrib. The most widespread javelin type among Celts, Greeks, Germans, and other Iron Age peoples. A versatile throwing weapon effective against unarmored and lightly armored targets.

Javelin, Iron Bodkin
  Prix : 18 sp
  Niveau Tech : 3 (Iron Age)

Spatha

Xiphos

  Niveau Tech : 3 (Iron Age)

Dory (Spear)
  Prix : 32 sp
  Niveau Tech : 3 (Iron Age)

War Pick
  Prix : 26 sp

Bow, Composite
  Prix : 28 sp

Composite Bow
  Prix : 52 sp
  Niveau Tech : 3 (Iron Age)

Quiver
  Prix : 10 sp

Celtic Sword

Akinakes

Javelin

Francisca

Throwing Knife

Plumbata

Sling

Flanged Mace
