APPLIED RULES (adapted for the Heartlands):
- Aketon worn under mail only (or as light standalone protection).
- Gambeson / Reinforced Gambeson for textile protections or worn under brigandine.
- Arming Doublet + Mail Voiders: EXCLUSIVE to kits with mobile plate pieces.
- War mounts require War Saddle for lance charges.
- The Heartlands are more "classic medieval" than the Sword Coast or the rich South. Less exotic equipment, more standard European-style gear.
- Dalelands have a strong militia tradition → many kits focused on light/medium infantry and archers.
- Impiltur has slightly more eastern influence but remains mostly standard.

TECHNICAL DISTINCTIONS:
- AKETON: Under-armor or light standalone.
- GAMBESON: Standard bulky quilted protection.
- REINFORCED GAMBESON: Best textile-only protection.
- PADDED JACK: Rigid standalone armor for heavy infantry.
- BRIGANDINE: Very common in the Heartlands.
- ARMING DOUBLET: Required when using articulated plate pieces.

All equipment names standardized to match the updated Groupe3 file.
