APPLIED RULES (adapted for the Northern Frontier):
- Aketon worn under mail only (or as light standalone protection).
- Gambeson / Reinforced Gambeson = primary protection in the North.
- Plate armor is rare and expensive. Most kits rely on mail + padded armor.
- Emphasis on practical weapons: axes, spears, longbows, and war hammers.
- Cold weather gear (heavy cloaks, furs) is more common in higher kits.
- War mounts are less common than in southern groups.

TECHNICAL DISTINCTIONS:
- AKETON: Light padded under-armor or standalone.
- GAMBESON / REINFORCED GAMBESON: Main protection for most northern fighters.
- PADDED JACK: Rigid standalone armor for heavy infantry.
- BRIGANDINE: Rare and expensive in the North.
- JAZERANT: More common here than in southern groups (lighter and more practical for rangers/scouts).

All equipment names standardized to match the updated Groupe5 file.
