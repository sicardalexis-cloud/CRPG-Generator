(Sword Coast + Baldur’s Gate + Luskan)
Tech Level: 6-7 (Early Renaissance → Late Renaissance)

================================================================================
USAGE RULES:
- All items Tech level 5 to 7 are **easily available** at the listed price.
- **Gunpowder weapons** have been **tripled** (x3) to reflect their rarity in the Forgotten Realms.
- Tech 8 = available but **very rare and expensive** (+50% to +100% surcharge possible).
- Prices in silver pieces (sp) unless otherwise noted.
- Availability: Always / Common = available in almost all major cities of the block.
- UPDATE:
1. Prices corrected for 15th century historical realism (strong increase on armors and weapons).
2. Integration of the Master Shield Matrix (Size 1 to 10, surface progression ×1.5).
3. Frequency/Bulk/Storage/Mounted columns applied to all protections.

DRAGON SCALE / DRAGON SKIN RULE (for Druid compatibility)
================================================================================

Dragon Scale or Dragon Skin versions can replace any normally metallic armor 
(Brigandine, Coat of Plates, Jack of Plates, Breastplate, etc.).
- Price is multiplied by **x4**.
- These versions do not disturb "green magic" (druidic magic).
- They are considered non-metallic for druid restrictions.

[PROJECTILE MECHANIC NOTE: "TROT D'ASSAUT"]
When moving towards a shooter in an "Assault Trot", the character lifts the 
top of the shield to lock the sightline line, systematically protecting FACE 
and NECK. This mechanical lifting, combined with the leg stride, shifts the 
coverage upwards: the lower torso, groin, and legs become exposed to incoming 
missiles as shield size decreases.

DETAILED SPECIFICATIONS & HIT-ZONE MAPS
================================================================================

SIZE 1 : Dual buckler
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* HISTORICAL REFERENCE: Ultra-light civilian fist buckler, often paired with a 
  side-sword or rapier for high-speed dueling and binding.
* CONTACT MODE HIT-ZONES: L_HAND, L_FARM
* ASSAULT TROT PROJECTILE COVERAGE: L_HAND, FACE
  (Note: Completely exposes the lower body; lifting to cover the face leaves 
  the entire torso vulnerable.)
* SYSTEM MODIFIERS: Active Defense D20: +1

SIZE 2 : Troop Buckler
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* HISTORICAL REFERENCE: Mass-produced iron or steel-faced wooden buckler. Standard 
  issue for English archers at Agincourt (1415) and 15th-century light infantry. 
  Easy to slip onto a sword hilt when operating a bow.
* CONTACT MODE HIT-ZONES: L_HAND, L_FARM, L_ELBO
* ASSAULT TROT PROJECTILE COVERAGE: L_HAND, L_FARM, FACE, NECK
* SYSTEM MODIFIERS: Active Defense D20: +1

SIZE 3 : Great Buckler
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* HISTORICAL REFERENCE: The absolute limit of center-grip design before weight 
  forces an arm-strapped configuration. Begins to transition in combat style 
  towards the coverage of a small targe.
* CONTACT MODE HIT-ZONES: L_HAND, L_FARM, L_ELBO, R_HAND (during cross-guards)
* ASSAULT TROT PROJECTILE COVERAGE: L_HAND, L_FARM, L_ELBO, FACE, NECK, CHST (Upper Left)
* SYSTEM MODIFIERS: Active Defense D20: +1 (+2 vs High/Overhead Melee strikes)

SIZE 4 : Small Target
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* PREHENSION MODE (Énarmé): First appearance of enarmes (leather arm straps). 
  At ~44 cm diameter, a full wooden/steel shield becomes too fatiguing for 
  a central fist-grip. Latched directly to the forearm, leaving the hand close 
  to the rim handle.
* HISTORICAL REFERENCE: Used by late 15th-century archers, urban night watches, 
  boarding mariners, and light cavalry. Direct ancestor of the Highland Targe.
* CONTACT MODE HIT-ZONES: L_HAND, L_FARM, L_ELBO, L_UARM, L_SHLD
* ASSAULT TROT PROJECTILE COVERAGE: L_HAND, L_FARM, L_ELBO, L_SHLD, FACE, NECK, CHST
  (Note: Upper torso is perfectly safe. ABDM, L_HIPS, and legs are fully exposed.)
* SYSTEM MODIFIERS: Active Defense D20: +2

SIZE 5 : Rotella
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* HISTORICAL REFERENCE: The definitive 50 cm civilian and elite guard shield. 
  The star of Renaissance Italian fencing treatises (Marozzo, Manciolino). 
  The hallmark of street duels, honor fights, and urban skirmishes.
* CONTACT MODE HIT-ZONES: L_HAND, L_FARM, L_ELBO, L_UARM, L_SHLD, CHST
* ASSAULT TROT PROJECTILE COVERAGE: L_HAND, L_FARM, L_ELBO, L_UARM, L_SHLD, FACE, NECK, CHST, ABDM (Upper half)
  (Note: Navel, hips, and lower limbs remain open targets.)
* SYSTEM MODIFIERS: Active Defense D20: +2

SIZE 6 : War Rotella
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* HISTORICAL REFERENCE: The pinnacle of battlefield round shields (57.5 cm diameter). 
  Core equipment for Spanish Rodeleros (targeteers) designed to sprint *under* 
  Swiss pike formations and exploit openings in close-quarters chaos. Built heavy 
  and proofed against early firearms ("à la preuve").
* CONTACT MODE HIT-ZONES: L_HAND, L_FARM, L_ELBO, L_UARM, L_SHLD, CHST, ABDM, L_HIPS
* ASSAULT TROT PROJECTILE COVERAGE: L_HAND, L_FARM, L_ELBO, L_UARM, L_SHLD, FACE, NECK, CHST, ABDM, L_HIPS
  (Note: Midsection locked down. GROIN and both thighs are exposed during stride phases.)
* SYSTEM MODIFIERS: Active Defense D20: +2 (Provides damage reduction against black powder pellets)

SIZE 7 : Heavy Assault Target
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* HISTORICAL REFERENCE: The Great Rondache of Breach. Conic, heavy steel-reinforced 
  beast built for trench warfare, siege breaches, and fortification storming. 
  Pure kinetic denial over swordplay ergonomics.
* CONTACT MODE HIT-ZONES: HEAD, FACE, NECK, L_SHLD, CHST, ABDM, L_HIPS, L_HAND, L_FARM, L_ELBO, L_UARM
* ASSAULT TROT PROJECTILE COVERAGE: All Contact zones + EYES, GROIN, L_THGH (Upper)
  (Note: Shooters must target KNEES and SHINS to bypass the wall.)
* SYSTEM MODIFIERS: Active Defense D20: +3 (Max active mobile bonus)

SIZE 8 : Hand Pavise
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* HISTORICAL REFERENCE: Asymmetric infantry marching pavise (~46x95 cm). Famous 
  for its use in Hussite line tactics and widely integrated by Genoese and 
  Burgundian mercenary crossbow units.
* CONTACT MODE HIT-ZONES: L_SHLD, CHST, ABDM, L_HIPS, L_THGH, L_HAND, L_FARM, L_ELBO
* ASSAULT TROT PROJECTILE COVERAGE: 
  * FRONT: FACE, NECK, L_SHLD, CHST, ABDM, L_HIPS, R_HIPS, GROIN, L_THGH, R_THGH, L_KNEE
    (Note: Alternately exposes L_SHIN/R_SHIN and feet at the base during strides.)
  * BACK RECHARGE MODE: BACK, LBAK (Passive protection while slung on spine)
* SYSTEM MODIFIERS: Active Defense D20: +2 (Suffers slight melee inertia malus)

SIZE 8a : Linden Round Shield (Tech 3 Alternative)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* HISTORICAL REFERENCE: Traditional early-medieval/Viking round shield (75 cm). 
  Central iron boss, light linden wood. High tactical area but low durability, 
  designed to shatter and absorb heavy blows over time.
* CONTACT MODE HIT-ZONES: L_HAND, L_FARM, L_ELBO, L_UARM, L_SHLD, CHST, ABDM
* ASSAULT TROT PROJECTILE COVERAGE: L_HAND, L_FARM, L_ELBO, L_UARM, L_SHLD, FACE, NECK, CHST, ABDM, L_HIPS, GROIN
  (Note: Shield projects a massive footprint but center grip makes it wobble

* SYSTEM MODIFIERS: Active Defense D20: +3 (Exceptional pivot mechanics)

* CONTACT MODE HIT-ZONES: L_HAND, L_FARM, L_ELBO, L_UARM, L_SHLD, CHST, ABDM, L_HIPS, L_THGH

SIZE 10 : Sturmpavise
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* HISTORICAL REFERENCE: Heavy fortification mantlet. Thick canvas-wrapped oak 
  planks built specifically to absorb the staggering kinetic force of heavy siege 
  bolts and long-range matchlock fire.
* CONTACT MODE HIT-ZONES: Non-usable due to weight and inertia constraints.
* ASSAULT TROT PROJECTILE COVERAGE: Not Applicable.
* PLANTED POSITION STATS: "Total Frontal Cover". Immunizes all 39 bodily zones 
  against direct line-of-sight front-arc projectiles for any character crouching, 
  engineering, or reloading behind its boundary.
* SYSTEM MODIFIERS: Active Defense D20: +0

SIZE 11 : Pavise
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* HISTORICAL REFERENCE: Full-scale wooden siege pavilion mantlet. Relies on
