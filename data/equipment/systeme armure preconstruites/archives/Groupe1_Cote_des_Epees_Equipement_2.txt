Niveau Tech : 6-7 (Early Renaissance → Late Renaissance)

RÈGLE D’UTILISATION :
- Tout objet de niveau tech 5 à 7 est **facilement disponible** au prix indiqué.
- Les **armes à poudre** ont été **triplées** (x3) pour refléter leur rareté dans les Royaumes Oubliés.
- Tech 8 = disponible mais **très rare et cher** (+50 % à +100 % supplémentaire possible).
- Prix en pièces d’argent (sp) sauf indication contraire.
- Disponibilité : Toujours / Commun = disponible dans presque toutes les grandes villes du bloc.
- MISE À JOUR :
  1. Prix corrigés pour réalisme historique XVe siècle (augmentation forte sur armures et armes).
  2. Intégration de la Master Shield Matrix (Taille 1 à 10, progression de surface ×1,5).
  3. Colonnes Fréquence/Encombrement/Stockage/Monté appliquées à l'ensemble des protections.

1. TEXTILE AND PADDED ARMORS
================================================================================

Aketon (basic padded jack)            8 sp    Tech 4   Always      Freq: 7  Bulk: 2  Storage: worn     Mounted: No  Description: A technical garment consisting of 4 to 8 layers of dense linen. Designed exclusively as an interface to be worn under a mail hauberk to prevent metal rings from pinching the skin and to absorb vibrations during impacts. Provides only weak protection against cutting edges.

Gambeson (basic)                      9 sp    Tech 4   Always      Freq: 8  Bulk: 2  Storage: worn     Mounted: No  Description: Standard padded tunic of 8 to 12 layers, often quilted in a diamond pattern. This is the base protection of medieval infantry, offering versatile resistance against light slashing blows and decent shock absorption.

Gambeson (long / fended)             14 sp    Tech 4   Common      Freq: 6  Bulk: 3  Storage: worn     Mounted: No  Description: An extended version of the basic gambeson reaching the knee. Often side-fended to allow for movement, it provides full leg protection for infantry who do not wear metal leg armor, sacrificing agility for coverage.

Reinforced Gambeson                  18 sp    Tech 5   Always      Freq: 7  Bulk: 2  Storage: worn     Mounted: No  Description: An improved version of the classic gambeson (12-18 layers) featuring reinforced padding at the shoulders and elbows. More rigid, it provides better protection against thrusting attacks while remaining supple enough to be worn under a plate breastplate.

Reinforced Gambeson (long)           26 sp    Tech 5   Common      Freq: 6  Bulk: 3  Storage: worn     Mounted: No  Description: The extended version of the reinforced gambeson. Combines high-density protection for the torso with heavy padding reaching the knees. Highly prized by professional soldiers who need reliable, standalone leg protection without the weight of steel chausses.

Padded Jack (medium / troop)         22 sp    Tech 5   Common      Freq: 6  Bulk: 3  Storage: worn     Mounted: No  Description: A very dense (20-30+ layers) standalone armor piece made of coarse linen. Specifically tailored for line infantry, it is designed to stop war arrows and polearm strikes, sacrificing mobility for maximum protection.

Coat of Plates                       45 sp    Tech 5-6 Common      Freq: 6  Bulk: 3  Storage: worn     Mounted: No  Description: A series of large steel plates riveted to the inside of a thick canvas or leather base. Intermediate protection offering superior torso rigidity compared to textiles while maintaining some articulated flexibility.

Brigandine (standard troop)          85 sp    Tech 5-7 Common      Freq: 5  Bulk: 4  Storage: worn     Mounted: No  Description: Armor composed of numerous small steel plates riveted to the inside of a robust garment. The overlap of the plates offers excellent protection against most weapons while conforming perfectly to the wearer's anatomy.

Brigandine (high quality)           240 sp    Tech 5-7 Rare        Freq: 3  Bulk: 4  Storage: worn     Mounted: No  Description: The noble version of the brigandine, utilizing high-grade hardened steel plates riveted with decorative brass or silver rivets. The outer fabric is often covered in silk or velvet, combining social prestige with lethal protection.

Jack of Plates                       55 sp    Tech 7   Common      Freq: 5  Bulk: 4  Storage: worn     Mounted: No  Description: A robust evolution of the jack, integrating horizontal steel slats inserted between the fabric layers. More affordable than a full brigandine, this is the preferred armor of men-at-arms and professional mercenaries.

Arming Doublet                       45 sp    Tech 7   Common      Freq: 6  Bulk: 2  Storage: worn     Mounted: No  Description: A technical masterpiece of textile armoring (12-18 layers). Precision-fitted, it features reinforced attachment points (points or aigillettes) allowing for plate armor pieces to be laced directly onto the wearer's body.

Pourpoint (light civil)              12 sp    Tech 6   Common      Freq: 7  Bulk: 1  Storage: worn     Mounted: No  Description: A light, fitted, padded civil garment. Although intended for fashion and comfort, its sturdy seams allow it to support a few light armor fastenings during travel or improvised urban defense.
