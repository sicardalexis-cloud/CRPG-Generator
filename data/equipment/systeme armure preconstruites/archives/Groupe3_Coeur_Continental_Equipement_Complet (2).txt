Common for archers and light infantry needing leg protection without metal armor.

Reinforced Gambeson                  18 sp    Tech 5   Always      Freq: 8  Bulk: 2  Storage: worn     Mounted: No  Protected Parts: CHST, BACK, ABDM, UARM, ELBO  Description: Improved 12-18 layer gambeson with extra padding at shoulders and elbows. Best textile-only protection.

Padded Jack		             22 sp    Tech 5   Common      Freq: 6  Bulk: 3  Storage: worn     Mounted: No  Protected Parts: CHST, BACK, ABDM, UARM, THGH  Description: Very dense (20-30+ layers) rigid standalone armor. Preferred by heavy infantry without plate.

Brigandine 		             85 sp    Tech 5    Common      Freq: 6  Bulk: 4  Storage: worn     Mounted: No  Protected Parts: CHST, BACK, ABDM, UARM, SHLD, AXIL  Description: Small steel plates riveted inside a robust garment. Excellent protection and flexibility. Common in the Heartlands.

Jack of Plates                       55 sp    Tech 7   Rare        Freq: 4  Bulk: 4  Storage: worn     Mounted: No  Protected Parts: CHST, BACK, ABDM, UARM  Description: Horizontal steel slats between fabric layers. Affordable alternative to full brigandine.

Arming Doublet                       45 sp    Tech 7   Rare        Freq: 5  Bulk: 2  Storage: worn     Mounted: No  Protected Parts: CHST, BACK, ABDM, UARM, ELBO, FARM  Description: Fitted technical garment with attachment points (aiglets) for mobile plate pieces. Essential when using vambraces, cuisses, etc.
