IMPORTANT NOTES:
- Base prices divided by 10 from community-voted prices.
- Adjustments made for a realistic 15th-century European economy (France-focused).
- 1 gp = 7 grams of pure gold ≈ 7–10 days wages for a skilled artisan.
- The Bead of Nourishment is kept at 3 gp (moderate economic impact outside military use).
- High-impact items have been raised to limit their effect on the medieval economy.
- A new section "WEAKER VARIANTS" has been added at the end with cheaper, less powerful alternatives.
- Items are sorted by ascending price within each main category.
