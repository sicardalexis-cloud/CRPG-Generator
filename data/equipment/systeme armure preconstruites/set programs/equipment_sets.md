# Equipment Sets — Sword Coast (Groupe 1)

**Setting:** Sword Coast + Baldur's Gate + Luskan
**Tech Level:** 6-7 (Early to Late Renaissance)
**Budget Range:** 275 sp to 684 sp
**Total Sets Generated:** 300

## Composition Rules Applied

- **Order of selection:** Weapons & Shield → Helmet → Body Armor → Rest of armor
- **Budget weapon progression:**
  - Low budget (≤75 sp): Spear + Shield dominates (poor militia)
  - Medium budget (76-400 sp): Shield + side weapon, OR bow/crossbow, OR polearm
  - High budget (401+ sp): Heavy armor + polearm (spear+shield archetype disappears)
- **Bow/Crossbow rule:** If equipped with a bow or crossbow, only a small shield (Size 3 or less) is allowed.
- **Nearly full armor rule:** If wearing 8+ armor pieces, shield is downgraded to Size 5 or less.
- **Shoulder-carried weapon:** Only ONE weapon carried on the shoulder (polearm, longsword, etc.) per set.
- **Besagews rule:** Besagews require both Pauldrons AND plate torso protection.
- **Pauldrons rule:** Pauldrons require plate torso (breastplate, brigandine, etc.).
- **Torso accessories rule:** Fauld, Garde-reins, and Tassets require plate torso.
- **Two-handed + Shield:** Characters with two-handed primary weapons CAN carry shields.
- Aketon can only be selected when wearing torso mail protection.
- Arming Doublet can only be selected when wearing torso plate protection.
- Gambeson / Reinforced Gambeson / Padded Jack / Pourpoint can be used freely as underlayers.
- Mail Chausses / Demi-chausses can be worn under plate leg armor or standalone.
- Mounted weapons (Lance, Light Hammer, Mace, Flanged Mace) are only included when a mount is present.
- Two-handed foot weapons are not assigned to mounted characters.
