EQUIPMENT SETS — Affluent_401-700sp
======================================================================

Budget range : 401-700 sp
Number of sets : 120
Source : generate_sets.py (random realistic kits with compatibility rules)
Region focus : Sword Coast / Côte des Épées (Groupe 1) — can be adapted

Rules summary (see generate_sets.py for full details):
  - Weapons & shield chosen first
  - Strict layering rules (pauldrons require cuirass/breast+backplate; besagews/tassets/fauld require plate torso, etc.)
  - Bows/crossbows limited to small shields
  - Mounts + barding appear at higher budgets
  - 'Nearly full armor' rule downgrades oversized shields
  - Side weapon (arming sword, falchion, messer or hand axe) bought before any armor
  - Gorget / Bevor always over at least fabric protection (aketon/gambison/arming doublet); never directly on skin. Often with mail collar underneath too.
  - Mounted weapons (Light Hammer, Mace, Flanged Mace, Lance) STRICTLY only when a mount is present.
    Foot characters (no mount) NEVER receive them — absolute, no exceptions even in low tiers.
